## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-20

First public release, by Shuvashis Gautam.

### Added

- Floating, transparent, always-on-top hookah that parks itself in the corner of the screen and remembers its position.
- Pull with the mouse, the Space key, or your hand: make a fist (or pinch) in front of the webcam to grab the hose.
- Recorded bubbling sound that follows how hard you pull, and a soft exhale.
- Thick WebGL smoke with adjustable amount and thickness.
- "My mouth" mode: a camera mirror where the exhale comes out of your mouth and follows your face.
- Optional smoke rings when you make an "O" while exhaling (My mouth mode).
- Rope-physics hose that slides off the vase and settles beside it.
- Clicks pass through the empty space around the hookah; tray menu with show/hide, settings, hand tracking, always on top, move to corner and quit.
- Self-healing camera that reconnects after drops instead of switching off; the camera is released while the app is hidden.
- Battery saver mode and an adaptive frame rate (60 fps while something moves, 5-8 fps when idle).
